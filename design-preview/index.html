What makes it unique

Satoshi + Instrument Sans

Satoshi for headings — geometric, clean, bold. Instrument Sans for body — crisp and legible. A modern pairing that feels confident without being loud.

Warm cream base, not cold white

#FEFCF8 base with paper-like grain texture. Feels handcrafted and approachable, not clinical.

Floating dark pill nav

Dark floating capsule instead of flat white bar. Feels premium, saves space, looks like nothing else.

Color-coded everything

Each stat card is a full pastel color, not white-with-icon. Each person gets their own pastel avatar. Status = color.

No borders, no shadows

Cards separated by color alone. Cleaner, calmer. Lead cards use a thin left accent bar for status.

Subtle grain texture

SVG noise overlay gives a paper-like organic feel. Very subtle. Makes it feel less digital, more human.

Tiny green dots, not "Live" badges

AI Brain knowledge items use a minimal green dot instead of a badge. Less noise, same information.
